# Developer Experience Pulse

A short questionnaire about the conditions that help developers make progress. Answer based on your experience during the last four weeks.

## Response scale

- **1:** Strongly disagree
- **2:** Disagree
- **3:** Neither agree nor disagree
- **4:** Agree
- **5:** Strongly agree

## Feedback loops

1. I receive useful feedback from local development tools quickly enough to maintain momentum. `[local-feedback-speed]`
2. Continuous integration results arrive quickly enough for me to act on them without losing context. `[ci-feedback-speed]`
3. Code review feedback is timely and helps me move the work forward. `[review-feedback-quality]`

## Cognitive load

4. It is clear how to set up, run, and test the services I work on. `[setup-clarity]`
5. I can find the technical information I need without searching across many disconnected places. `[information-discovery]`
6. Routine delivery tasks require a reasonable number of tools and manual steps. `[delivery-complexity]`

## Flow state

7. I have enough uninterrupted time to make meaningful progress on development work. `[focus-time]`
8. The expected outcome of my current development work is clear. `[work-clarity]`
9. My development environment helps me sustain momentum without avoidable interruptions. `[workflow-momentum]`

## Open questions

10. What is the single biggest source of friction in your development workflow today? `[biggest-friction]`
11. What one improvement would have the greatest positive impact on your daily work? `[highest-impact-improvement]`
